# Design Brief: idexi — Intelligent Event Solutions

This document outlines the design system, visual identity, and style guidelines for **idexi**, an AI-powered event solutions startup, based on the visual identity extracted from the brand logo.

---

## Color Palette

- **Primary Background**: Deep navy/indigo gradient (`#111d50` to `#29377b`), with subtle darker layered wave patterns for depth/texture.
- **Accent Gradient**: Bright cyan-to-blue gradient (`#31c4f3` to `#29377b`) used sparingly for highlights, buttons, hover states, or corner accents.
- **Primary Text/Icon Color**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for maximum contrast against the navy.
- **Optional Secondary Neutral**: Light gray (`#F2F2F2`) for off-background sections (like the logo's canvas).

---

## Typography

- **Logo Wordmark**: Bold, rounded geometric sans-serif (chunky, high x-height, friendly-yet-technical feel — similar to *Poppins Black*, *Century Gothic Bold*, or *Circular Bold*).
- **Tagline ("Intelligent Event Solutions")**: A lighter-weight version of the same sans-serif family for hierarchy contrast.
- **Website Pairings**:
  - **Headings**: Geometric sans-serif like *Poppins*, *Sora*, or *Manrope* (bold weights).
  - **Body Text**: Clean, readable sans-serif like *Inter*.

---

## Iconography & Visual Motif

- **Signal/Wifi Motif**: Concentric arcs representing "intelligence," connectivity, and broadcast/reach. Echo this motif subtly in website dividers, loaders, background elements, or icon sets.
- **Wave Motif**: Smooth, organic wave/liquid patterns layered in the background at low opacity. Reusable as section background textures.
- **Containment**: Circular/rounded containment (inspired by the logo sitting in a circle) suggesting soft-rounded UI elements (e.g., rounded buttons, rounded cards, rounded image containers).

---

## Overall Style Direction

- **Aesthetic**: Premium, futuristic, AI/tech-native, minimal but not flat. Implement depth via gradients and layered textures rather than flat design.
- **Mood**: Intelligent, dynamic, trustworthy, slightly luxurious (dark UI = premium feel).
- **UI Approach**: Dark-mode-first design, glowing/gradient accents on interactive elements, generous whitespace, rounded corners throughout, and subtle motion (waves, pulsing arcs) to reinforce the "intelligent signal" concept.
