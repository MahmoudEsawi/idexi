## Section 3 — Three Steps. Zero Searching.

**H2:**
```
Three Steps. Zero Searching.
```

**Layout:** Same horizontal 3-step connected flow pattern as the Pass page (linear, left-to-right, dashed connector lines) — this product's process IS sequential, unlike Flow's branching pattern. Coral accent color.

**The 3 Steps (icon + title + description):**

1. **Icon:** face/selfie icon
   **Title:** The Registration
   **Description:** Guests submit 4 details — name, email, and a face photo (a selfie or one from their gallery) — through a simple form. Organizers can collect this however fits best: a QR code at a dedicated idexi booth, on screen during the opening, or a link sent by email before or after the event.

2. **Icon:** sparkle/matching icon
   **Title:** The Matching
   **Description:** After the event, the organizer uploads the full photo folder — 10 photos or 10,000, it doesn't matter. Our own AI model, tested at 99% accuracy even in group shots, builds a private gallery for every single guest automatically.

3. **Icon:** mail/inbox icon
   **Title:** The Delivery
   **Description:** One click from the dashboard, and every guest's gallery is sent straight to their inbox.

### CRITICAL — accuracy notes for Saif (mechanics of the product)

1. **Who submits registration data:** The GUEST submits their own name, email, and face photo — not staff on their behalf. Collection happens through multiple possible channels that can run simultaneously: (a) a QR code at a dedicated idexi booth at the event, (b) a QR code displayed on screen during the event opening (with hosts encouraging guests to scan it), and/or (c) a form link sent by email before or after the event. These are not mutually exclusive — an organizer can use all of them at once.

2. **Who uploads the photos:** The EVENT ORGANIZER uploads the full raw photo folder after the event — not the photographer directly, and not automatically. This is a manual upload step done through the dashboard.

3. **AI accuracy figure:** "99% accuracy" refers to idexi's own custom-built facial recognition model (not a third-party/off-the-shelf model), tested specifically against crowded event scenarios including group shots. This exact figure is also used in the homepage's floating FAQ widget — keep it consistent everywhere it appears.

4. **No specific processing/delivery time claims** — see note in Section 2 above. Do not imply "seconds" or "minutes" for the AI matching step without a verified benchmark.
