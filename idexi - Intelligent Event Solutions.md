Idexi Face
| Your Event Photos, Delivered Instantly

The Core Concept:
It's simple: Attendees should not have to search through thousands of event
folders to find themselves. Idexi Face automatically finds every guest's
photos and sends them directly to their phone.
How It Works (In 3 Simple Steps):
1. The Selfie: Guests register by taking a quick selfie and providing their email.
2. The Sorting: Event photographers upload all the raw photos to our system.
Our smart software instantly scans the pictures and matches faces.
3. The Delivery: Guests receive an email with a link to their own personal
photo gallery. No searching needed.

Idexi Pass
The Core Concept:
Say goodbye to manual ticket approvals and generic emails. Idexi Pass is a
high-speed automation tool that sends beautifully branded, personalized
digital QR tickets directly to your guests in minutes.
How It Works (In 3 Simple Steps):
1. The Guest List: You simply upload an Excel sheet with your attendees'
details (Name, Category, etc.), or have them register directly through our
platform.
2. The Automation: With one click, the system generates thousands of unique
QR codes.
3. The Delivery: In under 5 minutes, every guest receives a professional email
ticket containing their name, specific category, and their personal QR code.

Idexi Flow
The Core Concept:
You don't need expensive scanning hardware or messy printed lists. We turn
your staff's personal smartphones into powerful tracking tools. With just one
universal QR code per guest, you control every touchpoint of your event.
How It Works (Dynamic Scanning):
You can give secure dashboard access to any number of organizers or
volunteers. They simply use their phones to scan a guest's QR ticket for
different purposes:
Access Control: Instantly verify entry times and highlight VIP guests.
Logistics & Hospitality: Track exactly who received their welcome kit,
badge, or lunch meal to perfectly manage your inventory.
Workshops & Sessions: Verify which specific workshop a guest
registered for and guide them to their assigned seat number.
